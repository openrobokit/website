Models and Policies

GraspPolicy in src/graspkit/models/grasp_policy.py maps an observation to a grasp: three hand poses (pre-grasp, grasp, post-grasp) that the trajectory builder turns into an executable motion.

Open and closed loop use the same policy. The execution controller decides when to observe and predict again; the benchmark's closed-loop mode refreshes the observation and replans at step 75.

The policy selects CVAE from models/cvae.py or FlowMatching from models/flow_matching.py using generative_model (cvae, flow, flow_transformer). Both generators expose forward, decode and sample; access the selected component as policy.generator. No shared base class is needed.

Saved configuration field names and the checkpoint's cvae.* parameter keys are unchanged. The policy registers its generator under that historical checkpoint name, while callers use generator. Existing state dictionaries load strictly without migration. Imports use GraspPolicy, GraspPolicyConfig and build_grasp_policy; the training CLI subcommand is agent:grasp_policy_config.

The CVAE

text
point cloud (B, N, 3)
   -> [yaw canonicalization / augmentation] -> [centering] -> vision encoder (SpUNet or PointNet)
        |                                            |
        | global feature                             | per-point features
        |                                            +-> objectness head  (target vs background)
        |                                            +-> graspness head   (where to grasp)
        v
   CVAE encoder (training: ground-truth actions) -> z -> decoder -> (B, n, 3, d) actions

d is the hand's joint count for a fixed base; a floating base prepends a translation and a rotation, [xyz, rotation, hand joints], where the rotation is either Euler rxyz or a 6D representation. The three queries are pre-grasp, grasp and post-grasp, in that order.

At inference the encoder is skipped: the graspness head picks the top-k points, and the CVAE decodes a latent per point — the mean latent when use_mean, otherwise a sample scaled by cvae_temperature. With use_mean the diversity comes from the distinct query points, not the latent. grasp_evaluator_guidance_steps instead refines the latent by ascending a frozen grasp evaluator inside a ball of radius grasp_evaluator_guidance_radius.

The vision encoders include a sparse-convolution U-Net (vision_encoder="spconv", the default, voxel size 5 mm), a BN-free PointNet and RGB CNN/ResNet variants. RGB construction and shared training/inference preprocessing live in models/vision_encoder/rgb.py; the policy keeps state/camera conditioning and generator selection. The CVAE is an MLP encoder/decoder over the flattened action vector, conditioned on the vision feature, optionally with a learned prior.

Flow matching uses the same visual conditioning and action representation. Its velocity network is an MLP (flow) or transformer (flow_transformer), trained along a linear noise-to-action path. Sampling integrates eight Euler steps and restores physical joint units using the bank's action mean/std. Flow returns zero latent moments through the shared training interface, so its KL contribution is zero; joint reconstruction uses MSE.

The last two need scene geometry at training time; the trainer builds it only every penetration_every steps and after penetration_warmup_iters.

Representation flags

  • convert_rxyz_to_rot6d — 6D rotation inside the CVAE, decoded back to Euler. analytic_euler picks the closed-form decode over the iterative one.
  • coupled_floating_base — all three queries share the grasp rotation, and grasp and post-grasp share its translation.
  • center_pointcloud — predict translations relative to the cloud centroid.
  • canonical_yaw — rotate into a deterministic yaw frame derived from the cloud's second moment.
  • train_yaw_augmentation — random training-time yaw applied to points and targets together.

Data

GraspDataset reads the processed HDF5: per scene, one rendered view is drawn uniformly per access, points are sampled (object-only or whole scene), and actions are sampled per grasp. With graspness enabled, the per-action best points are placed first and the rest sampled around them. Graspness labels come from the contact extractor's palm-cone weighting during process_sim_data. Fresh flow models use meta/action_mean and meta/action_std to normalize their action space. CVAE training uses raw poses.

Training

AdamW at lr=1e-4, weight decay 1e-4, a 10x step decay two thirds of the way through, DDP when distributed. Every step scans the gradients and skips the update if any rank saw a NaN or Inf. Checkpoints hold {"agent": state_dict}; the provenance keys the benchmark expects (experiment, policy_config, training) are added by the packaging scripts. Evaluation is described in Sim deployment.

From prediction to motion

A predicted grasp is not executable on its own. GraspTrajBuilder applies the filters (hand-axis cone, contact-avoid, penetration, rest pose), optionally IK-converts a floating grasp onto an arm with an orientation gate and an arm-range band, and plans the approach, closing, holding and lift segments. The benchmark runs the filters; deployment skips them and relies on the IK gate, since a real capture has no ground-truth object pose.
